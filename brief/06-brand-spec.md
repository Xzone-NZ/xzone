## Colour (form)
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary green | #16B800 | Logo, Relief range |
| Black | #000000 | Logo, Sports range |
| Navy | #00176D | Joints range |
| Relief accent | #E3FF2F | Relief range |
| Joints accent | #FFA726 | Joints range |
| Sports accent | #FF1D00 | Sports range |
| Text | #171717 (Coal) | Guidelines, form blank |
| Background / tint | White + light green tint | Guidelines, form blank |

**Removed from guidelines:** Bush Deep #113619, Bush #1E4D27, Sun #FBCD0A, Ember #E8471F, and the "no blue, no navy" rule.

**Range system:** each range uses its own pair:
- Relief: green #16B800 + lime #E3FF2F
- Joints: navy #00176D + orange #FFA726
- Sports: black #000000 + red #FF1D00

The guidelines' "yellow range pill" becomes the range accent pill.

**Contrast to watch:**
- White text on #16B800 is about 2.6:1, too low for headlines and body.
  - On green grounds, use black text, or put white text on black or navy.
  - Use #16B800 for the logo, rules, pills and large shapes, not small text.
- #E3FF2F on white is unreadable. Use it on black or navy only.
