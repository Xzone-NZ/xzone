## Social design direction (feedback, 12 Nov)
Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/RLoAHdj2FRV5R3hstYRMWL (Round 1: three directions; Round 2: refined).
- **Approved:** Reel 1, the founders story ("Why we swapped the office for the Waitākere Ranges"), and Story 1, the ankle photo with a poll sticker.
- **Headlines are always hooks.** Write every reel, story and post headline like the Reel 1 one: a curiosity or story hook ("Why we…", "The one thing we…", "Every family has one…"), with a play-button "Watch…" line on reels. Voice rules still apply (no exclamation marks, no "cures").
- **House style:** Reel 2's green #0D441F and lime #E0E64A with Poppins ExtraBold. Key words get a lime (or range-accent) highlighter.
- **Stories:** Story 1's format, a full photo plus a two-option poll and a "tomorrow we share…" line.
- **No red or orange on social** (12 Nov). Use green, lime and white, with navy kept only for Joints pieces. Orange and red stay on packaging only.
- **Posts:** Post 1's layout (photo on top, review in a band below) in lime (green text), white (green or navy text) or green (white text, lime label).
- **Less dark.** The first round was too dark. Mix in white and lime grounds and don't run more than one dark-green design in a row.
- **Pending photos:** backyard cricket, Hooker Valley track, bush walk, and two hands close-ups were added to Round 2 but are not approved yet.

## Carried over from guidelines (form silent so far)
These are the guidelines' rules. Each one is replaced if a later part of the form answers it.
- Logo use, clear space, minimum sizes, avatar (black icon on white circle)
- Photography: place, people, product. Natural light. No stock, no studio, no AI people.

## Voice (form part 2, with guidelines where silent)
- Personality: **authentic, trustworthy, empowering, informative, generous** (replaces "honest, outdoorsy, knowledgeable, warm")
- Write as **we**. NZ spelling.
- Humour rarely, never political or sexual. Slang only where it fits.
- Let customers do the selling: surprise that it works, "the only one that works for me".
- Never name a competitor negatively. A general contrast with pharmaceutical options is fine.
- **No emoji anywhere** (stricter than guidelines). Hashtags fine on all channels (keep the guidelines' five-max habit on Instagram).
- From guidelines: teach first, te reo greetings, name the herbs, "supports / helps / traditionally used", never "cures", no price-only posts, no exclamation marks in headlines.

## Channels and cadence (form part 3)
- Facebook and Instagram first. YouTube for corporate video. LinkedIn and TikTok not a priority.
- **Cadence: 3 posts a week**, cross-posted to FB and IG.
- Monthly asset mix: 4 Reels, 4 single-image posts, 4 carousels (IG), 12 Facebook posts, 3 email newsletters, 1 campaign email, testimonial graphics ("we would love this"). Stories and highlight covers: not a priority.
- Channel-specific tone is fine. Jess and Dan happy on camera and doing voiceovers.
- Scheduling in Meta Business Suite. Approvals: Jess and Dan, fast.
- Visual references they like: Antipodes, Harkers Herbals. Dislike: Voltaren.

## Proof (form part 3)
- Public reviews (Judge.me product pages, Google Reviews) and customer emails can be quoted, full names where the customer used them publicly.
- Lab (Trinity Bioactives, 2024, in vitro): Relief 42.7%, Joints 48.1%, Sports 54.1% reduction in macrophage concentration; control indomethacin 30%. Client wording includes "proven to decrease pain-associated cells" and "almost twice as effective as" indomethacin. **Decision (11 Nov): always say "in independent lab tests". Do not use "proven" or present the indomethacin comparison as a product claim.**

## Story (form part 3)
Friends since primary school in West Auckland, aged six. Waitākere Ranges. Jess (commercial real estate) and Dan (advertising) left corporate jobs. Bought the business from a horse trainer whose race-horse rub became a human product, "Comfort Zone". Retail X research, a rongoā course, reformulated from two native herbs (harakeke, kūmarahou) to eleven across three ranges.
