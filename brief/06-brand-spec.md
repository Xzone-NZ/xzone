## Final look: price-change carousel (approved 25 Nov)
The four-slide carousel "5 years. Same prices." (Five years same prices, Why we've held off, Our commitment to quality remains unchanged, What's changing) is the approved look and feel for feed posts and carousels. It replaces the earlier highlighter-block treatment. Reference: the four slide PNGs supplied 25 Nov (1080 x 1350).
- **Layout:** photo fills the top 650 px; a solid logo-green #004F23 panel below carries the text. White type on green.
- **Headline:** Poppins ExtraBold (about 70 px at 1080 wide), white, sentence case, one hook per slide, at most two short lines. One hand-picked key word carries a **lime #E0E64A underline (about 8 px) that sits behind the letters**, so descenders break through ("changing", "held off", "unchanged", "Same prices"). Never a lime highlighter block, never a lime-coloured word.
- **Supporting text:** Poppins Medium or Regular (about 32 px), white, under the headline. Detail lives in the caption, not on the image.
- **Callout bar:** a lime rounded bar with green Poppins Bold text for the one line that needs weight.
- **Bullets:** lime circle with a dark green tick, white text.
- **Button:** dark green pill with white text and a lime arrow.
- **Date badge:** lime top strip, dark green body, white numerals, lime year, at the photo/panel join on the right.
- **Swipe cue:** "SWIPE" in white Poppins with a lime arrow, bottom left of slide 1 only.
- **Logo:** white X-ZONE New Zealand lock-up, bottom right of the green panel, **on every slide of a carousel** so a reader opening on any slide sees the brand.
- **Never put the logo over a person's face.** In any photo with people, keep the logo clear of faces (it sits in the green panel).
- **Photo choices:** real NZ places and people, natural light. Native flowers (kōwhai, pōhutukawa/rātā) suit herb and values slides; a founders photo suits the "why" slide.

## Standing artwork rules (client feedback, 12 to 25 Nov)
- Lime underline behind the key word, never a highlighter block, never lime-coloured text.
- Logo on every slide of a carousel.
- Never place a logo over a person's face.
- Licensed stock photography is allowed. Keep the licence and file ID with the artwork. (The guidelines' "no stock" rule is removed, 19 Nov.)
- No red or orange on social; no AI-generated people; no white studio shots.

## Voice (form part 2, with guidelines where silent)
- Personality: **authentic, trustworthy, empowering, informative, generous**
- Write as **we**. NZ spelling.
- Humour rarely, never political or sexual. Slang only where it fits.
- Let customers do the selling.
- Never name a competitor negatively. A general contrast with pharmaceutical options is fine.
- **No emoji anywhere.** Hashtags fine on all channels (five max on Instagram).
- Teach first, te reo greetings, name the herbs, "supports / helps / traditionally used", never "cures", no price-only posts, no exclamation marks in headlines.

## Channels and cadence (form part 3)
- Facebook and Instagram first. YouTube for corporate video. LinkedIn and TikTok not a priority.
- **Cadence: 3 posts a week**, cross-posted to FB and IG.
- Monthly asset mix: 4 Reels, 4 single-image posts, 4 carousels (IG), 12 Facebook posts, 3 email newsletters, 1 campaign email, testimonial graphics.
- Scheduling in Meta Business Suite. Approvals: Jess and Dan, fast.
- Visual references they like: Antipodes, Harkers Herbals. Dislike: Voltaren.

## Proof (form part 3)
- Public reviews (Judge.me, Google Reviews) and customer emails can be quoted, full names where the customer used them publicly.
- Lab (Trinity Bioactives, 2024, in vitro): Relief 42.7%, Joints 48.1%, Sports 54.1% reduction in macrophage concentration. **Decision (11 Nov): always say "in independent lab tests". Do not use "proven" or present the indomethacin comparison as a product claim.**
