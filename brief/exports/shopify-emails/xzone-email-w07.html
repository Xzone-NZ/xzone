DOMS, explained

The soreness that turns up two days after a big session is DOMS, delayed onset muscle soreness. New or harder work leaves tiny strains in muscle fibres, and repairing them takes time. It is not lactic acid. What helps: keep moving gently, drink water, eat some protein, sleep well and massage the sore muscles. A sudden, sharp pain at the back of the thigh is different: stop and see a physio or GP.
