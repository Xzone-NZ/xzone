Twenty seconds before backyard cricket

Most backyard cricket injuries happen in the first over: cold muscles, a big lunch and one heroic dive. Ten arm circles each way. Six walking lunges each side, with a twist. Side shuffles across the lawn, then three easy run-ups before you bowl flat out. If you go down hard and something swells fast, get it checked by your GP or an after-hours clinic.

The day after

Boxing Day aches are often less about the cricket and more about the heat and too little water. Water first: a big glass when you wake, then keep sipping. Then move: a slow walk or a swim. Then a rub on the sore spots before bed, and a proper sleep. If cramps are severe, or you feel dizzy or confused, see your GP or call Healthline on 0800 611 116.
