The sideline bag

School's back, so Saturday sport is back, and so are the knocks. Pack it once and leave it in the car: water, snacks, an ice pack, a bandage, plasters and sunscreen. Knocks and bruises: cold for 15 minutes, then a gentle massage once the swelling settles. Rolled ankles: off the field, rest, ice, a firm bandage, and no more play until it is pain-free.

Head knocks are different

If your child has a knock to the head and seems dazed, sick, sleepy or not themselves, take them off and see a doctor. When in doubt, sit them out. And before trials, a 20-second warm-up helps: high knees, side shuffles, calf raises, then small hops landing soft with knees over toes.
