Your bruise colour is a clock

A bruise changes colour as it heals. Red to purple: day one or two, so a cold pack in short bursts helps. Blue to green: days three to six, when gentle warmth and light massage around the spot can help. Yellow to brown: about a week in, nearly there. See your GP if a bruise appears for no reason, is very large, sits over a joint you can't move, or isn't fading after two weeks.
