The first 48 hours

Long weekends bring long drives, new tracks and backyard games. Rolled ankle: rest it, ice wrapped in a cloth for 15 to 20 minutes, a firm bandage, and keep it raised. Bumps and bruises: cold first, then a gentle massage once the swelling settles. Stiff after the drive: stop every two hours to walk and stretch. If you can't put weight on it, see a doctor or call Healthline on 0800 611 116.

Five minutes before you start

Most weekend sports niggles start in the first ten minutes, on cold muscles. A brisk walk or easy jog, ten arm circles each way, leg swings front to back, then a few gentle build-ups to speed. Afterwards, walk it off and stretch while you are still warm. Sharp pain that stops you mid-game is a signal to stop, not to push on.
