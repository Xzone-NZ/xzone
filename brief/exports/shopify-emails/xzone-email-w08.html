Heat or ice

Ice is for new injuries: a fresh sprain, a knock or anything swelling in the first 48 hours. Wrap it in a cloth, 15 to 20 minutes, never straight on the skin. Heat is for stiffness: a tight lower back, a sore neck, muscles that ache after a long day. Morning joint stiffness often prefers warmth. A joint that is suddenly hot, red and swollen is worth a GP visit.

Shoulders after overhead work

Ceilings, gutters, painting and pruning leave the muscles around the shoulder tight and tired. At the end of the day, massage a rub into the top and back of the shoulder for a minute, then finish with slow arm circles. If your shoulder catches with pain, will not lift above your head, or the ache wakes you at night, see your GP or physio.
