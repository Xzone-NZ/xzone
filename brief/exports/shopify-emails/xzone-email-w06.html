Why joints stiffen

Joint stiffness is often worst first thing in the morning and after sitting still: the long drive to the bach, an afternoon in the deck chair. Gentle movement keeps joints comfortable and the muscles around them strong, so the best answer to stiffness is usually little and often, not rest.

Three habits, and the 10% rule

Move little and often: short walks beat one long one. Warmth for morning stiffness: a warm shower before you get going. Keep the muscles around the joint strong with gentle exercise. And if the new year means more walking, gardening or training, add no more than about 10% a week. If a joint is suddenly hot, red and swollen, or the pain keeps you up at night, see your GP.
