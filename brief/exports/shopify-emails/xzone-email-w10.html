Three sleep habits

Your body does most of its repair while you sleep, and no product beats a good night. Keep the same wake time every day, weekends too. Make the room cool, dark and quiet, with the phone out of reach. Give yourself thirty minutes of wind-down off screens. A slow massage into whatever aches is a good way to tell your body the day is over.

When a medicine and aches arrive together

Some common medicines are linked to muscle aches as a side effect, including statins for cholesterol, some antibiotics and longer courses of steroid tablets. It doesn't happen to everyone. The important thing: don't stop a prescribed medicine on your own. Write down when the aches started and talk to your GP or pharmacist. Check with your pharmacist that a rub suits you alongside your medicines.
