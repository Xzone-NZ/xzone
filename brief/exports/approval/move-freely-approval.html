X-ZONE New Zealand · Email plan for approval

Move Freely

Our new email newsletter, named after your tagline. Thirteen emails from late November to mid-February: nine newsletters and four campaigns. Each one is shown below exactly as it will look in an inbox.

Why "Move Freely"

It is the first half of "Move Freely. Live Naturally.", so it reads as X-ZONE before anyone sees the logo. It also says what the emails are for: practical lessons that help people keep moving.

What every email has

  • The Move Freely masthead, issue number and date
  • A hero photo, headline and one clear button
  • "In this issue", then short, numbered lessons
  • The week on Instagram and Facebook
  • Your sign-off with a photo of you both
  • A real Judge.me review, and the range

How to approve

Scroll through each email. Under "Please confirm" are the facts only you can give us, such as dates, offers and stock. Reply with your yes, or with changes by email number (for example "No. 07: change the button to ...").
