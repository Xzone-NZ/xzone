# Review: X-ZONE Email Signature (draft)

Draft: https://claude.ai/artifact/MmXp2cYwedpcEExEPPweyT
Three versions (joint, Jess, Dan). Table-based HTML, Arial, tap-to-call numbers, logo linked to xzone.nz.

## What works
- Built properly for email clients (tables, inline styles, Arial). Install steps for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail.
- Short and clean. Numbers formatted and tap-to-call.
- "Ngā mihi mahana" with the macron fits the NZ native, rongoā story.
- The "How to use" link to the instruction video is a nice touch.

## Fix before sending

1. **Not the brand colours.** It uses dark green #1E4D27, yellow #FBCD0A for the divider and grey-green #5C6B60. The brand is green #16B800 and black #000000. Suggest:
   - Divider rule: #16B800 (in place of yellow)
   - Name: #000000 (the #16B800 green is too light for small text on white, about 2.6:1 contrast)
   - Website link: #16B800 bold is fine at this size, or keep it black
   - Secondary text: a neutral grey such as #555555
2. **Logo URL not checked.** The HTML loads `https://xzone.nz/cdn/shop/files/X-Zone_Logo_Landscape_Green.png`. It couldn't be checked from this session. The Drive file is called `X-Zone_Landscape_Green.png`, so the name may be a guess. Upload the PNG to Shopify > Content > Files and paste in the real URL, or the logo will show as a broken image.
3. **Tagline is not theirs.** "Backed by science. Powered by plants." isn't in the brief. The client's lines are **"Move Freely. Live Naturally."** or **"Move Freely, Naturally."** (and the 2026 tagline lock-ups say "Move Freely" / "Live Freely"). Use theirs, or ask before introducing a new one.
4. **Facebook dropped, but it's their biggest channel.** The note says "Instagram is the channel that matters". Facebook has 2.5k followers to Instagram's 656, and the older Joints audience lives there. Keep both: `Facebook · Instagram`.
5. **Unconfirmed details.**
   - Names: the form says "Jess Martin" and "Dan Holt". Check they want "Jessica Martin" / "Daniel Holt" in full.
   - The `@xzonenz` handle and Instagram URL: social handles were left blank on the form, so confirm them.
   - Consider adding `sales@xzone.nz` to the joint version. Forwarded emails lose the From line, and stockists need it.
