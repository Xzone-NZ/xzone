Day two on the track

Day two is when the track catches up with you. A hot spot on your heel: stop, dry the skin and tape it before it becomes a blister. A blister that is intact: leave it whole and cover it. Tight calves: stretch at every break and massage them properly at the hut. See your GP if a blister shows spreading redness, swelling, pus or heat.
