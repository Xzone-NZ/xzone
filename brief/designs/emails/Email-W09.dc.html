Tennis elbow, explained

Tennis elbow is pain on the outside of the elbow, and most people who get it have never held a racquet. It comes from the same small movement repeated: gripping a drill, painting a ceiling, pruning. What helps: ease the load by changing grip or swapping hands, build strength back gently once the sharp pain settles, and massage the forearm morning and night. If it came from a fall, or your hand is numb or weak, see your GP.

Five free habits for your back

Warm up for two minutes before the first lift. Keep the load close and turn with your feet. Change position every 30 minutes. Ask for a hand with the heavy stuff. Look after the drive home and your sleep. Back pain with numbness, weakness, pain down a leg, or any change to your bladder or bowel needs your GP straight away.
