They produce topical, natural, herbal pain relief rubs, sprays and roll-ons for muscles and joints.

## What makes them different

1. **More actives:** 11 to 14 active ingredients per SKU, where most competitors use 5 or 6.
2. **NZ native botanicals:** the only brand using 11 New Zealand healing herbs traditionally used for pain and inflammation, as used by Māori healers in rongoā rākau (Māori plant medicine).
3. **Lab tested:** the only natural brand with its 3 rubs (Relief, Joints, Sports) independently in vitro lab tested, by Trinity Bioactives. Results showed reduced macrophage inflammatory cells compared with the control (Indomethacin).
4. **Natural, low harm:** suitable for sensitive skin.

**Why customers choose them:** it works. Testimonials regularly mention reduced pain and inflammation where competitors gave little or no relief. Growing reputation for chronic conditions and high pain levels.

## Ideal customer

People with longer-term chronic pain and/or higher pain levels who prefer natural (non-pharma) topical relief. They may have tried pharmaceuticals out of desperation and found relief short-lived. Active, outdoorsy, want to stay mobile and live life to the full. Defined more by that shared desire than by demographics.

- Activities: nature walking, travelling, camping, gardening, tennis, golf
- Often played sport when younger and now live with resulting injury pain
- Typical age: 40 to 85

**Problem:** They want to keep living a full, active, exploratory life but feel held back by pain or mobility issues.

**Question before buying:** "I suffer from (X). Will X-ZONE help with this?"

**Main objection:** They have tried competitor or pharmaceutical products with little or short-lived effect, so they doubt X-ZONE will be any different.

## What has worked

- Mid-October 4-day sale, 20% off: about $1.5k
- Labour Day one-day **Buy 2 Get 1 Free:** 60+ orders, about $4k. Best sale to date. Supported by 3 emails plus social announcements in the lead-up.

Takeaway: a short, urgent, bundle-style offer with an email build-up beat a longer percentage discount.

### Lab results (their wording, "Awards")
Independent in-vitro lab tests by Trinity Bioactives (2024):
1. Relief Rub: reduced macrophage concentration by 42.7%. "Therefore, proven to decrease pain-associated cells."
2. Joints Rub: reduced macrophage concentration by 48.1%. "Helping to reduce joint pain by targeting and decreasing inflammatory cells."
3. Sports Rub: most effective, reduced macrophage concentration by 54.1%. "Almost twice as effective as the Control, Indomethacin, which is 30% reduction."

### Story (their words, summarised)
- Friends since they met at primary school in West Auckland, aged 6. Grew up with the Waitākere Ranges on their doorstep.
- Corporate backgrounds: Jess in commercial real estate, Danny in advertising. Wanted something more meaningful.
- Bought the business from a horse trainer who made the original product for race horses' tight, sore muscles. Friends and family kept asking for "that horse stuff", so she made a human version under the brand **Comfort Zone**, the precursor to X-ZONE.
- After buying it: market research with **Retail X**, did a **rongoā course**, and reformulated. The original had only harakeke and kūmarahou. They added the 11 NZ natives plus extra herbals to create the three ranges.
