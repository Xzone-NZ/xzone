# X-ZONE review library

Every customer review supplied so far, **word for word** (typos and all), with a claims verdict. Source: X-ZONE's Judge.me reviews, pasted by the client on 12 Nov 2026. Product is taken from the review or from X-ZONE's reply. Where a reviewer line was missing from the paste, the name comes from X-ZONE's reply.

**Rules for using a review**
- Quote it word for word. Never shorten, tidy or "fix" a customer's words, and never stitch two reviews together. If a short excerpt is ever needed, Jess and Dan approve it first.
- Show the name as the customer gave it, plus "Judge.me review" (or "Verified Judge.me review" only where Judge.me shows Verified).
- A customer may name a condition in their own words, but our caption or headline never repeats it as a claim.
- Run the Claims Check skill on the piece the review sits in.
- **Reviews from 2018 mention Comfort Zone**, the product before it was reformulated into the 11-herb X-ZONE range. Use them only for brand history ("customers have loved it for years"), not as proof for a current product, unless Jess and Dan agree.
