## What works
- Herb of the week across 11 weeks is a strong, ownable spine. No competitor can copy it.
- "Promo anchored in education" suits the main objection ("I've tried things that didn't work").
- The dates line up: Christmas Fri 25 Dec, Boxing Day Sat 26 Dec, Auckland Anniversary Mon 1 Feb, Waitangi Day Sat 6 Feb.
- Clear weekly rhythm and story formats. Easy to hand to a scheduler.

## Must fix before it goes to the client

1. **Wrong core customer.** The brief's ideal customer is 40 to 85, chronic or high pain, Joints Rub as a flagship. The plan leads with summer sport, kids and tradies. Joints doesn't really show up until week 8. Joints and the older Facebook audience need a regular weekly slot from week 1.
2. **The client's sale dates are missing.** The brief says: price rise mid Nov, prices discounted back to the old price for a month, then a **pre-Christmas sale from mid Dec**. The plan has Black Friday (week 1) and a bach-cupboard bundle (week 4), but no "old price for a limited time" message and no clear pre-Christmas sale.
3. **The 80/20 split vs the revenue goal.** The goal is NZ$20k to $40k a month within 3 months. Their best result ever was a one-day Buy 2 Get 1 Free backed by **3 emails**. The plan has no email and only one soft promo a week. Keep 80/20 on the organic grid, but add an email track and Meta ads (NZ$600/month) for each sale moment.
4. **Claims risk (NZ therapeutic advertising).** These need to come out or be checked:
   - "The lab rated [Tūpākihi] closest to a drug" and the "COX-2 number". The brief's lab result is **in vitro**, on the **whole rubs**, measuring macrophage inflammatory cells against Indomethacin. It wasn't per herb and doesn't show the rubs work in people. Present it as an in vitro lab finding, never as matching a medicine.
   - Kids' age guidance ("Relief from five, Joints and Sports from seven, not in pregnancy"). Use exact label wording only.
   - The "is your pain really dehydration" and "seven medicines" posts must stay general information with "see your GP".
5. **Facts that don't match the brief.** The plan says 186 stockists, the brief says 200+. The 2026 numbers (carbon offset, sugarcane packaging, countries) aren't in the brief. Check the 11-herb list and which range each herb is in against the ingredient labels.
6. **DONE 11 Nov.** Poppins only, 200+ stockists. Colours stay on the guidelines palette (form hex codes were wrong).

## Check with the client
- **Capacity.** 78 feed pieces, 26 Reels and daily stories, many with Jess and Dan on camera. The on-camera and asset-quantity questions on the form were left blank. Confirm before committing to 2 Reels a week.
- **Ambassadors** (Katrina Amiria, Annika Hogguer Mearns, Maya Grundy, Jasmine Marsters, Mollie Allison, Otago Boys 1st XV, Steve Wilbury): do we have permission and current photos?
- **Rongoā / te reo content.** Who reviews the cultural content (weeks 5 and 11 especially)?
- **The "subscription" (week 8) and "tradie pack" / "Recovery Kit" / "sideline kit" bundles.** Do these exist in Shopify?
- **Wholesale.** Half the 6-month goal is stockists. Only week 9 speaks to them. Consider a monthly stockist and practitioner (physio, chiro, massage) post.

## Suggested tweaks
- Swap one sports post a week for a **Joints / chronic pain** post (arthritis, sciatica, mobility at 60+, "I've tried everything" stories). Facebook first.
- Make the weekly **testimonial** a feed post on Facebook, not just a story. Proof is the answer to the main objection.
- Add an **email + ads layer** for: Black Friday/old-price ends (wk 1 to 3), pre-Christmas sale (wk 4), Boxing Day (wk 5), New Year Recovery Kit (wk 6).
- Track sales from each promo moment, not just saves. The client measures success in dollars.
