## Meet X-Zone, Jess & Dan

Our first muscle recovery formulation was originally created for high-performance horses, due to a lack of natural, effective alternatives at the time. It was developed to assist racehorses and performance horses to recover from muscle soreness and injury, particularly following competition.

We didn't test on animals but we did test on humans! Friends and family tested the formulation for efficacy… and loved the results. After repeated requests for "that stuff for the horses", the evolution into human products began.

Further experimentation, tests and trials led to the X-ZONE range of products for humans. Years of testing led to a unique combination of anti-inflammatory, pain-relieving ingredients that really worked. Then came the development of three different formats – rubs, roll on and spray – to make application easier. Our team are truly passionate about creating natural products that work to provide effective pain relief for the everyday New Zealander.

### Our values and what makes us happy

We've been friends since we met at primary school in West Auckland at 6 years old. Growing up with the beautiful Waitakere Ranges on our doorstep has given us both an appreciation for nature and its healing properties.

We're committed to helping our customers to optimise their lifestyle. There's no better feeling than hearing our products are genuinely improving lives or enabling peak performance!
We get out of bed every day with the aim of helping people to rise above their pain, re- experience well-being and achieve their life goals – achieving this makes us happy.

## Notes for using it

- **Matches the brand spec story:** friends since age six in West Auckland, the Waitākere Ranges, the horse rub that people kept asking for, the Rongoā course, then more native herbs. Use it as the source for the founders Reel and the "Our story" highlight.
- **Dan's message is the best voice line we have.** "We're certainly not so arrogant to think our products can remove all types of pain permanently" is honest and fits the "supports, helps, never cures" rule. Good for an About Reel, a founders carousel slide or an email PS.
- **Jess and Dan photos to pair with it** are in the client's Drive (Jess and Dan in X-ZONE tees, in the garden, at an expo, Jess with horses, Dan with his guitar). See `brief/photos/README.md`.
- **Claims to check before reusing on social or email:** "a unique combination of anti-inflammatory, pain-relieving ingredients" and "nature and its healing properties" are therapeutic-style claims. Run them through Claims Check; on social, prefer "native herbs traditionally used for pain".
- **Spelling:** the page writes "Waitakere"; use the macron, Waitākere, in new copy. "Rongoā" already has it.
- **Bio lines:** "Animal lover & nature enthusiast" (Jess) and "Rhythm guitarist and amateur philosopher" (Dan) are good, human details for captions and the founders Reel.
