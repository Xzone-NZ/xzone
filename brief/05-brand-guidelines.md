# X-ZONE Brand Guidelines v1 (Nov 2026, prepared by Reclaim Co)

Source: `source/xzone-brand-guidelines-v1.pdf` (2 pages).

## Brand line
**Backed by science. Powered by plants.** Always these words, this order, full stops included. Supporting proof point beneath it: "11 NZ healing herbs".
Positioning: Natural pain relief, made in Aotearoa.
Personality: honest, outdoorsy, knowledgeable, warm. Never clinical, never hype.

## Logo
- Primary: green on white. Reversed: white on Bush Deep, or on a photo with a green wash. Icon: the koru X, used alone.
- Clear space = height of the X. Minimum width 30 mm print / 120 px screen, below that use the icon.
- Social avatar: black icon on a white circle, no ring.
- Watermark: icon at 8 to 12% opacity on green.
- Never recolour, outline, shadow, stretch, or place on a busy photo without the wash.

## Colour (60% green / 30% white / 10% accent)
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bush Deep | #113619 | Primary ground |
| Bush | #1E4D27 | Bands, pills, links |
| Sun | #FBCD0A | One accent per layout |
| Ember | #E8471F | Sports range only, small |
| Mint | #E2F5EB | Light tint, panels |
| Coal | #171717 | Body text |

Rules: one yellow element per layout. **No blue, no navy.**

## Type
- Display: Poppins ExtraBold / Bold, sentence case, leading 1.05
- Sublines and names: Poppins SemiBold
- Body: Open Sans Regular, 1.45 leading, SemiBold for emphasis, never italic
- Labels: Open Sans Bold, uppercase, 1.6 pt tracking, 7 to 8 pt
- Fallback: Arial in email signatures and Office docs
- Sentence case everywhere. All caps only for short labels.

## Photography
Place (NZ bush, water, light through canopy), People (Jess, Dan, customers, athletes, real not stock), Product (in the hand or in use, never floating). Natural light. Show the body part. Text on photo: Bush Deep wash 15% top to 75% bottom. No gym stock, no white studio, no AI-generated people.

## Voice
- Teach first. Plain, warm, short sentences, Kiwi not corporate.
- Te reo greetings are part of the voice: Kia ora, Ngā mihi, Aotearoa.
- Name the eleven herbs.
- "Supports", "helps", "traditionally used". Never "cures". No "clinically proven" unless the independent test is linked.
- No price-only posts. No exclamation marks in headlines. No emoji in body copy.
- **80/20:** four in five posts educate, one in five sells (anchored in a reason to use).

## Instagram system
1. Avatar: black koru X on a white circle.
2. Green-ground post: bush photo under a green wash, white logo top left, yellow range pill top right, Poppins ExtraBold headline bottom left with a yellow subline, footer with handle and site.
3. Photo-led post: full-bleed photo, green title band at the top (white headline, yellow subline).
4. Alternate 2 and 3 down the grid.
5. Reels: filmic, slow moves, cross-dissolves, grain, few words, no voiceover unless Jess or Dan. Cover follows the green-ground style.
6. Captions: first line is the lesson, product last. Five hashtags max, Aotearoa and herb names first.
